About the Artist

I'm Chinmay.


I spend most of my time building things with code, sitting in front of screens, and navigating logical frameworks.


But sometimes, I put the keyboard away, step back into the physical world, and pick up a pencil or watercolor brush.


This digital sketchbook is a quiet room built to archive these charcoal studies, pencil drafts, and painting experiments over the years.
